Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FitTrack is a personal workout-tracking app. This page explains what data it collects, how it's stored, and what choices you have.

What FitTrack stores locally

Your exercises, weekly routine, workout sessions, and body weight logs are stored on your device, in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). This works fully offline and applies whether or not you create an account.

What FitTrack stores in the cloud

If you create an account to sync across devices, the same workout data above — plus your email address, used for sign-in — is stored on our backend provider, Supabase. This only happens if you choose to sign up; using the app without an account ("Skip for now") means nothing ever leaves your device.

When you delete something while signed in, the record itself is removed from the cloud. A small marker — the record's random ID and the time it was deleted, with none of its contents — is kept so your other devices know to remove their copy too.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics or advertising trackers
  • No ads
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties, other than Supabase acting purely as our hosting/database provider

Your choices

  • Use the app entirely offline, with no account, by choosing "Skip for now"
  • Export a backup of your data at any time from Settings
  • Sign out of your account at any time from Profile
  • Request deletion of your account and cloud-stored data by contacting us (below) — this doesn't affect data already stored locally on your device
  • Clear your browser's site data for this app to remove everything stored locally

Children's privacy

FitTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
